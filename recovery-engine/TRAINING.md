# Recovery Engine: training pack (for Rahul)

## 1. SM one-pager (print one per SM)
**Your job in the app:** decide people and hours. The app tells you where the variance is and who your best auditors on the floor are.

**Every day, 10:00 to 11:00:**
1. **Open the link** (bookmark it). You see only your store. "Data 10:05" at the top shows how fresh it is.
2. **Read the top card:** store state (BROKEN / DETERIORATING / CLEAN), the recommended recovery (location check, rack sweep or aisle reset) and why.
3. **Racks with most variance:** tap **Do first** on the racks you want covered today. They go to your best captain before anything else.
4. **Crew:** your most accurate auditors **on the floor today** are already ticked ("On floor · 10:42", "In today · 07:15"). Untick, change hours or add a name freely. **Nobody will ask you why.**
5. **Confirm plan → Make printable sheet → print.** Give each captain their own page.
6. **On the HHD:** create the location audits listed on each page and assign them to that captain. For rack sweeps, create every location in the rack.

**End of the shift:**

7. **Close the shift:** actual hours per captain, and each task done / partial / not done. Two minutes. Without it your store shows **NO ACTUALS** to your CM.

**Next day:** Store results shows suggested vs planned vs actual hours, how many planned locations were really audited (from audit data), and ₹ recovered per man-hour.

**Do:** log real hours, even when they are fewer than planned. **Don't:** confirm a plan you won't work. Re-plan instead ("Re-plan today" replaces it).

## 2. CM one-pager
- **Cluster review, twice a day (11:30 and 18:30).** It shows only your stores; the stores that need you are sorted to the top.
- **What the flags mean, and the usual action:**

| Flag | Meaning | Usual action |
|---|---|---|
| NO_PLAN | No plan today | Call the SM: plan before 11:00 |
| NO_ACTUALS | Yesterday's shift not closed | SM closes it today |
| LOW_EXECUTION | Audit data shows under 70% of planned locations audited | Check the HHD audits were created and assigned |
| LOW_ADHERENCE | Under 70% of planned hours were worked | Was the crew pulled to picking? Plan inside the low-order window |
| NO_YIELD | Hours spent, nothing recovered yet | Check quality gaps; move sweeps to the more accurate captains |
| RELAPSED / WORSE | Store variance went back up after recovery | Escalate (SLP), or ask for a full reconciliation if aisles are broken |

- **Click a store** to see its latest plan and evidence. **Record decision**: the decision, an owner and the next review date. Every decision is logged.
- **Adding an SM:** one row in the USERS tab (email, SM, outlet_id). It works at once.

## 3. Rahul's 45-minute SM session (per region, on a video call)
| Min | What |
|---|---|
| 0–5 | Why: recovery hours are the most wasted hours in the store; the app puts them where the ₹ is, with the people who count accurately |
| 5–20 | Live demo on one SM's real store: read the card, Do first on a rack, the crew, confirm, print |
| 20–35 | Every SM opens **their own** store and walks steps 1–5 (confirm only if they will work the plan today) |
| 35–40 | Close-the-shift demo, and what the CM sees |
| 40–45 | Questions + checklist: link bookmarked, can see own store, knows how to print, knows who to call |

**Trainer checklist (before the session):** your own USERS row as CM or PM; the SM list for the region in USERS; the link; one store with fresh data (Monitoring shows a good refresh in the last hour); the SM one-pager PDF.

## 4. SOP: Rahul's task
The printed "How" steps come from the **SOP** tab: level (LOCATION / RACK / AISLE / SEARCH / PILFERAGE), step number and text. The current rows are a draft written without seeing the HHD. Replace them with the ground SOP. The next printed sheet uses them; no code change needed.

## 5. FAQ
- **"The crew ticked someone who isn't here."** Presence comes from today's audits, up to the time shown. Untick them; nobody asks why.
- **"My store isn't in the list."** Your USERS row has the wrong outlet_ids. Ask your CM.
- **"It says the feed refreshed, reload."** New data arrived while you were planning. Reload and confirm again (about 30 seconds).
- **"Printed page has too many items."** It lists up to 10–15 items per task as a watch-list. For sweeps, count every unit at every location.
- **"Why was this rack marked unstable?"** Parth's rule: ≥25% of its locations are direct targets (slow picks or repeated corrections), or ≥50% show any signal, or it holds 4 or more variance items.
